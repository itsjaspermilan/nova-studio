Kenney assets used by examples/pine-ridge-rally's menus, HUD and interface sounds (all Creative Commons Zero, CC0 1.0; credit is welcome,
not required). From the "Kenney Game Assets All-in-1 3.8.0" bundle (https://kenney.nl/assets), bought by the project's owner:

- UI Pack (2.0): sprites/ui/btn_yellow.png, btn_grey.png, divider.png
- UI Pack - Adventure: sprites/ui/panel_dark.png, panel_frame.png, bar_green.png
- Input Prompts (Nintendo Switch set): sprites/ui/pad_*.png (the handheld labels its buttons like a Nintendo pad)
- Interface Sounds: sounds/menu_move.ogg (tick_002), menu_accept.ogg (confirmation_002), menu_back.ogg (back_002), pause.ogg (open_001)
- Impact Sounds: sounds/bump.ogg

Each pack's own License.txt (CC0, http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) is in its folder of the bundle. The files are copied
by scripts/rally/kenney-ui.sh.

- Fonts (Other/Fonts): fonts/future.ttf (Kenney Future) and fonts/future_narrow.ttf (Kenney Future Narrow), CC0, used for the HUD's small labels and hints.
- Music Loops (Music Loops/Loops): music/kenney_menu.ogg (Swinging Pants), kenney_results.ogg (Cheerful Annoyance), kenney_forest.ogg (Time Driving),
  kenney_desert.ogg (Mission Plausible), kenney_snow.ogg (Flowing Rocks), kenney_night.ogg (Space Cadet). Chosen by their titles, not yet listened to.
- Fonts (Other/Fonts): also fonts/thick.ttf (Kenney Thick, the logo), fonts/bold.ttf (Kenney Bold, headings) and fonts/rocket.ttf (Kenney Rocket).
- sounds/lap.ogg (Interface Sounds, confirmation_003) and sounds/best_lap.ogg (Music Jingles, Steeldrum, jingles-steel_07).

Nebula Swap (examples/nebula-swap), all CC0 from the same bundle, copied by scripts/nebula/assets.sh:
- UI Pack - Sci-fi: sprites/ui/btn_blue, btn_yellow, btn_grey, glass, metal, dot, bar_* (the buttons, panels and bars of the menus)
- Input Prompts (Nintendo Switch set): sprites/ui/pad_*.png
- Planets and Skyboxes Space (shrunk): sprites/bg/planet*.png, nebula.png, galaxy.png; Simple Space: sprites/bg/star_*.png
- Interface Sounds, Digital Audio, Sci-Fi Sounds: sounds/*.ogg
- Music Loops: music/menu.ogg (Space Cadet), play.ogg (Infinite Descent), versus.ogg (Alpha Dance), puzzle.ogg (Mission Plausible), over.ogg (Game Over)
- Fonts: fonts/future.ttf and future_narrow.ttf (Kenney Future)
The six blocks, the garbage plate, the cursor and the ring are drawn by scripts/nebula/gems.mjs (original, MIT like the rest of the repository).

Potion Panic (examples/potion-panic), all CC0 from the same bundle, copied by scripts/potion/assets.sh:
- 3D models (converted by scripts/kenney.mjs): Mini Dungeon (chest, barrel, table, potion, character-human, ...), Food Kit (the ingredients, pot-stew as the cauldron), Nature Kit (trees, flowers, rocks,
  mushrooms, cactus, tent, campfire), Graveyard Kit (pine, gravestone, candles, lanterns, pumpkin, crypt, fence), Fantasy Town Kit (the stall as the serving hatch, lantern)
- Input Prompts (Nintendo Switch set) as pad_*.png. The other interface pictures (panels, buttons, discs, stars, arrows, flasks, bars) are drawn by
  scripts/potion/hd-ui.mjs, and the ingredient icons are renders of the Food Kit models above (scripts/potion/hd-ui.sh)
- Sounds: Interface Sounds, RPG Audio, Impact Sounds, Foley Sounds (water, woosh), Digital Audio. The music is original (scripts/potion/music.mjs), MIT like the rest of the repository
- Fonts: the built-in Cinzel (headings) and Lora (text), SIL OFL

Moving Day (examples/moving-day), all CC0 from the same bundle:
- 3D models (scripts/moving-day/assets.sh, converted by scripts/kenney.mjs): the Furniture Kit (sofa, chairs, TV cabinet and screen, bed, plant,
  bookcase, lamp, fridge, stove, desk, table, box, bathtub, washer, floors recoloured for the rooms, walls, windows, doorways, rugs) and the Mini Characters
  (the mover and the clients). Each has an editable models/<name>.model.json made by the studio's importer, except the characters and the plant (listed
  in studio/src/tools/keep-original.txt), checked against the kit's files by scripts/moving-day/check-models.sh
- Input Prompts (Nintendo Switch set) as pad_*.png. Every other interface picture is drawn by scripts/moving-day/hd-ui.mjs, and the wish card's icons and
  the clients' faces are renders of the models above (scripts/moving-day/hd-ui.sh)
- Sounds (scripts/moving-day/sounds.sh): Interface Sounds, Impact Sounds, Foley Sounds (stone drag, woosh), RPG Audio (creak, page), Digital Audio, a
  Music Jingles pizzicato. The music is original (scripts/moving-day/music.mjs), MIT like the rest of the repository

Sunny Hop (examples/sunny-hop), all CC0 from the same bundle:
- New Platformer Pack (1.1): every picture of the game is rasterised from its vectors (Vector/Tiles, Characters, Enemies, Backgrounds) by
  scripts/sunny/art.mjs: sprites/tiles.png, hero, friends, enemies/*, boss/* (the pack's enemies drawn three times as big), items/*, bg/*,
  ui/hud_* and ui/gem_*. Its sounds (Sounds/sfx_*.ogg) are sounds/jump, jump_high, coin, gem, hurt, bump, magic, disappear, select, throw, splash.
- Input Prompts (Nintendo Switch set): sprites/ui/pad_*.png
- Impact Sounds, Digital Audio, Interface Sounds, Retro Sounds 2, Music Jingles (Steeldrum, jingles-steel_06) and the Voiceover Pack (female:
  ready, set, go, new_highscore): sounds/*.ogg, copied by scripts/sunny/sounds.sh (its list says which file is which).
The panels, buttons, stars, the stopwatch, the pointer, the crown, the skies' gradients and the particle pictures are drawn by
scripts/sunny/art.mjs (original). The music (music/*.song.json, rendered to .ogg) is written by scripts/sunny/music.mjs (original).
