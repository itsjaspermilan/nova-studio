Kenney assets used by examples/pine-ridge-rally's menus, HUD and interface sounds (all Creative Commons Zero, CC0 1.0; credit is welcome,
not required). From the "Kenney Game Assets All-in-1 3.8.0" bundle (https://kenney.nl/assets), bought by the project's owner:

- UI Pack (2.0): sprites/ui/btn_yellow.png, btn_grey.png, divider.png
- UI Pack - Adventure: sprites/ui/panel_dark.png, panel_frame.png, bar_green.png
- Input Prompts (Nintendo Switch set): sprites/ui/pad_*.png (the handheld labels its buttons like a Nintendo pad)
- Interface Sounds: sounds/menu_move.ogg (tick_002), menu_accept.ogg (confirmation_002), menu_back.ogg (back_002), pause.ogg (open_001)
- Impact Sounds: sounds/bump.ogg

Each pack's own License.txt (CC0, http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) is in its folder of the bundle. The files are copied
by scripts/rally/kenney-ui.sh.

- Fonts (Other/Fonts): fonts/future.ttf (Kenney Future) and fonts/future_narrow.ttf (Kenney Future Narrow), CC0, used for the HUD's small labels and hints.
- Music Loops (Music Loops/Loops): music/kenney_menu.ogg (Swinging Pants), kenney_results.ogg (Cheerful Annoyance), kenney_forest.ogg (Time Driving),
  kenney_desert.ogg (Mission Plausible), kenney_snow.ogg (Flowing Rocks), kenney_night.ogg (Space Cadet). Chosen by their titles, not yet listened to.
- Fonts (Other/Fonts): also fonts/thick.ttf (Kenney Thick, the logo), fonts/bold.ttf (Kenney Bold, headings) and fonts/rocket.ttf (Kenney Rocket).
- sounds/lap.ogg (Interface Sounds, confirmation_003) and sounds/best_lap.ogg (Music Jingles, Steeldrum, jingles-steel_07).

Nebula Swap (examples/nebula-swap), all CC0 from the same bundle, copied by scripts/nebula/assets.sh:
- UI Pack - Sci-fi: sprites/ui/btn_blue, btn_yellow, btn_grey, glass, metal, dot, bar_* (the buttons, panels and bars of the menus)
- Input Prompts (Nintendo Switch set): sprites/ui/pad_*.png
- Planets and Skyboxes Space (shrunk): sprites/bg/planet*.png, nebula.png, galaxy.png; Simple Space: sprites/bg/star_*.png
- Interface Sounds, Digital Audio, Sci-Fi Sounds: sounds/*.ogg
- Music Loops: music/menu.ogg (Space Cadet), play.ogg (Infinite Descent), versus.ogg (Alpha Dance), puzzle.ogg (Mission Plausible), over.ogg (Game Over)
- Fonts: fonts/future.ttf and future_narrow.ttf (Kenney Future)
The six blocks, the garbage plate, the cursor and the ring are drawn by scripts/nebula/gems.mjs (original, MIT like the rest of the repository).
