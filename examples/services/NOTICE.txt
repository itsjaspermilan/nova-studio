awake.png, blink.png and chime.wav are original procedural fixtures authored
for Nova Studio, without third-party artwork, recordings or samples.
Reproduce them using /usr/bin/python3 scripts/generate-service-assets.py from
the repository root; --check verifies the existing bytes without writing files.

The two geometric sprites and synthesized PCM chime are dedicated to the
public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal; see licenses/CC0-1.0.txt in distributions.
This dedication applies to these three assets, not the Nova source-code license.
The scene and generator source follow the Nova source-code license.

WAV contract: mono signed PCM16, 16000 Hz, 2560 frames (0.16 seconds), exactly
RIFF/WAVE fmt(16) then data, generated from a faded 440/660 Hz sine pair.
There are no metadata chunks, external files or decoder dependencies.
